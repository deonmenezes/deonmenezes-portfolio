    How it works

    Jev, TypeSafe AI's decision model, reads your post and estimates how likely a typical reader is to like it, reply, repost, open your profile, stick around, hit "not interested", or report it.

    Those estimates are scored with the weights X published in its open-source ranking algorithm ↗:

    Reply
    +13.5
    Profile click
    +12
    Opens and stays 2+ min
    +10
    Repost
    +1
    Like
    +0.5
    Not interested / block
    −74
    Report
    −369

    The weights are real. The views and likes are a simulation for fun, not a promise. Not affiliated with X, TypeSafe AI, or Vercel.
