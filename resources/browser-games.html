Keep in mind

Three things before you play.

  • These are fan ports.None is made or approved by the games' publishers, and any of them can go offline the way GTA 5 and the Call of Duty ports did. They are other people's sites, not mine.
  • What I checked.I opened every link again on 23 October 2026 and took off the ones that stopped working: the Call of Duty ports (two of their sites now show DMCA notices, the rest no longer load a game) and Hedgewars, which did not answer. A page answering does not prove the game runs on your device.
  • From the list's author."Some need your own game files. Skip anything that asks you to download a launcher."
