How to play GTA 6 early (the New Zealand trick)

GTA 6 comes out on November 19 and unlocks at midnight local time, so New Zealand gets it first. On PS5 you can start at New Zealand's midnight with a New Zealand PlayStation account. Here are the steps, and when that is where you live.

See the PS5 steps When it unlocks for you

PS5 steps

Five steps on PS5.

On PS5, changing the console clock alone does not do it. You need a separate account whose region is New Zealand, and the game bought on that account.

  1. 01

    Make a PlayStation account set to New Zealand

    On your PS5, create a new PlayStation Network account and set its country or region to New Zealand.

  2. 02

    Go to the New Zealand PlayStation Store

    Signed in to the new account, head to the PlayStation Store, which is now the New Zealand store.

  3. 03

    Buy GTA 6 on the New Zealand store

    You can pay with your usual payment method, but Insider Gaming says the safest way is a New Zealand PlayStation Store gift card.

  4. 04

    Pre-load it from November 10

    Pre-loading opened on November 10 for digital pre-orders. Rockstar says the download is up to 212 GB, so clear space first.

  5. 05

    Play at New Zealand midnight

    The game unlocks at 00:00 on November 19 in New Zealand, which is 11:00 UTC on November 18. Find your own time in the table below.

Extra step

Play it on your main profile.

If you want to play on your own account instead of the New Zealand one, do this right after you make the New Zealand account.

  1. 01

    Turn on Console Sharing and Offline Play

    Go to Settings > Users and Accounts > Other > Console Sharing and Offline Play and set it to Enable.

  2. 02

    Switch back to your main profile

    When GTA 6 unlocks, switch back to your main PS5 profile. Because sharing is on, you can play the game there early too.
