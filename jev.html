curl

  -H "Authorization: Bearer YOUR_DJEV_KEY" \
  -H "Content-Type: application/json" \
  -d '{
    "state": "I was charged twice. Please refund the extra charge.",
    "questions": {
      "wantsRefund": {
        "type": "boolean",

  }'

JavaScript

Reference

The three question types

boolean
A yes/no question. You get probability, from 0 to 1, that the answer is yes. criteria is optional: { "true": "...", "false": "..." }.
choice
Pick one option. criteria maps each option to a short description. You get the winning choice and probabilities for every option.
score
Rate on a scale. criteria is a list of levels, lowest first (at least two). You get a score where 0 is the first level, and it can land between levels.

Tip: ask small, specific questions. Ten narrow questions in one request beat one vague one, and they all run in parallel.

Fine print

Limits and errors

  • 1,000 requests a day per key, resetting at 00:00 UTC. Up to 10 questions and 32 KB per request.
  • 3 keys a day per network. Keep your key out of public code; anyone holding it spends your daily limit.
  • 401 means a missing or unknown key. 400 means the request shape is off, and the message says where. 429 means a daily limit was hit.
  • This is a free community service that runs on my own account, with no uptime promise. If it takes off, limits may change.
  • Your text is sent to Vercel AI Gateway and TypeSafe AI to get the answer. I store request counts per key, not your text.
